Countermeasure Pyramid

RCA Reference Guide — Safety Hierarchy of Controls

🎯 Goal: Aim for Level 4 or higher! The pyramid shows the hierarchy of countermeasure effectiveness. Higher levels (toward the top) create more sustainable, long-term solutions. Lower levels are easier to implement but less effective long-term.

📋 Quick Reference — Pyramid Levels & Due Dates

Level Name Description Due Date
Level 1 Eliminate Remove the hazard entirely from the workplace 120 days
Level 2 Automate Replace manual process with automation 90 days
Level 3 Error Proof Design the process so mistakes are impossible 25 days
Level 4 At a Glance Visual controls — make the correct way obvious 5 days
Level 5 Verify Checklists or audits to confirm compliance 5 days
Level 6 Remind Signs, notices, or verbal reminders — least effective 3 days
